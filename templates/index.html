CSV format & validation rules

Required columns, in this order:

order_id,date,customer,product,category,quantity,unit_price,status

Dates: YYYY-MM-DD. Unique order IDs: letters, numbers, hyphens or underscores. Quantity: 1–9999. Unit price: non-negative USD, up to two decimals, no currency symbols. Status: Paid, Pending or Refunded. All fields are required and limited to 80 characters. UTF-8 and comma-separated values only. All orders belong to one USD report.
